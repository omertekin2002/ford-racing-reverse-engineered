PRESS PLAY TO START

Click the game to focus the keyboard. Press Esc to leave fullscreen.

Get on the grid

Press Enter to continue past the introduction. Use the arrow keys to select menu items, Enter to confirm and Esc to go back. Select Quick Race to drive the demo’s car and track.

Take control

Arrow keys accelerate, brake and steer. Space is the handbrake, Right Ctrl gives a boost, Enter changes the camera and P pauses. Game settings adjusts detail and language. Save with OK, then press Play demo.

Keep your settings

Game saves and settings stay in this browser on this device. Private browsing or clearing site data may remove them. Music and video streaming are disabled in this preview.
